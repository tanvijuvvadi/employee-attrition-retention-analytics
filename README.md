# Employee Attrition Prediction & Retention Analytics

## HR Analytics | People Analytics | Employee Retention | Power BI | Excel | SQL

---

## Project Overview

Employee attrition is one of the major workforce challenges faced by organizations across industries. High employee turnover can increase recruitment costs, training expenses, productivity loss, knowledge loss, and workload pressure on existing employees.

This project, **Employee Attrition Prediction & Retention Analytics**, is an industry-oriented HR Analytics project designed to analyze employee workforce data and identify patterns associated with employee attrition.

The project combines:

- HR Analytics
- People Analytics
- Employee Attrition Analysis
- Employee Retention Analysis
- Workforce Analytics
- Employee Engagement Analysis
- Compensation Analysis
- Career Growth Analysis
- Risk Scoring
- SQL Analysis
- Excel Analytics
- Power BI Dashboard
- HR Retention Strategy

The project uses **synthetic employee data only** and does not contain real employee information.

---

# 1. Business Problem

Organizations need to understand why employees leave and which workforce segments require further investigation.

Traditional HR reporting often focuses on basic headcount and employee turnover numbers. However, HR Analytics can provide deeper insights by analyzing multiple workforce factors together.

This project addresses questions such as:

- Which departments have higher attrition?
- Which job roles experience elevated employee turnover?
- Which tenure groups show higher attrition?
- Is overtime associated with employee exits?
- Are low-engagement employees leaving more frequently?
- Is career stagnation associated with attrition?
- Does salary progression appear related to employee turnover?
- Which exit reasons occur most frequently?
- Which workforce segments require HR investigation?
- Which retention interventions should HR consider?
